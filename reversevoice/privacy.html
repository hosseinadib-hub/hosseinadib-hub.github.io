Privacy Policy

Reverse Voice: Backwards Game

Effective date: November 4, 2026

In short
  • Your voice recordings never leave your phone.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
  • The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and does not use the internet.

This policy explains how Reverse Voice: Backwards Game ("the app"), made by Brightloop ("we"), handles information. It applies to the app on Android, published on Google Play as com.brightloop.reversevoice.

Microphone and voice recordings

The app asks for microphone access because recording a word and an imitation is how the game is played. Recordings are kept only in your phone's memory while a round is being played, so they can be reversed and played back. They are discarded when you start a new round or close the app.

Recordings are never saved to files, uploaded, or shared with us or anyone else. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send them anywhere.

Music you choose

You can pick a song from your phone to play as background music. The app only plays that file on your phone. It remembers your choice (a reference to the file and its name) on your phone so it can play it again next time. It never copies or uploads the file.

Settings stored on your phone

The app saves a few settings on your phone: your language choice, whether you have seen the tutorial, and your chosen music. These never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party tracking. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers or any other personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that change how information is handled, such as online play, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
